# Jog wheels and scratch transitions

Hold a deck's jog wheel with a mouse or touch and move around it to scratch the loaded audio forward or backward. Holding still holds the record. Near the spindle, straight finger movement controls the scratch instead of an unstable angle. Release restores the previous playing/paused state. Scroll over the wheel to seek; Shift increases the seek step. Grabbing a wheel hands control to the DJ and stops Auto DJ automation.

DJ → Transition options → **Scratch transitions** enables short scratch bridges for large tempo gaps and an occasional two-stroke flourish during Auto DJ (every fourth completed handoff). The next deck must already be ready. The scratch releases before crossfading, and the configured mix duration remains unchanged. Disable the toggle for clean transitions. Stopping automation cancels a pending scratch bridge.

This is playback manipulation, not a change to source music, beatgrids, or saved cues. It is not a beat-quantized scratch sequencer or hardware timecode implementation.
