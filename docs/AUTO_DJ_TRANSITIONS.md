# Auto DJ transition styles

Open DJ → Transition options. Enable **Auto DJ transition effects**, choose Variety, Scratch, Stutter, Tape brake, or Clean fade, and set how often to use an effect (every 2–12 mixes). Variety rotates Scratch, Stutter and Tape brake; the default is every fourth mix. These choices are saved on this computer and keep the existing crossfade duration.

Scratch uses a short outgoing jog phrase before the handoff. Stutter briefly repeats a short outgoing segment, and Tape brake slows the outgoing audio during the last part of the crossfade, with the incoming deck already playing. Effects are skipped when the outgoing deck is not ready or has a user loop active. Variety and Scratch also retain the short scratch bridge for large tempo gaps.

Stopping automation, taking manual jog control, disabling effects or completing a handoff releases temporary scratching and stutter looping. Original audio, saved cues and permanent deck pitch settings are unchanged. These are lightweight playback effects, not an AI turntablist or phrase-perfect scratch performance. Android does not yet expose these transition controls.
